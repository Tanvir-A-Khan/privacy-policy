Privacy Policy

One Path

Last updated 2026-10-10

Game: One Path (Android) · Developer: Tanvir Ahmed Khan (ttech.devstudio) · Effective: 10 October 2026

One Path works fully without an account. This page explains what we collect if you create an account or use the optional online features, why, and your choices.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address and passwordYou create an account with emailTo sign you in and let you recover your progress. The password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.
Google account email and nameYou choose "Continue with Google"To create and sign in to your account. We receive only your email address and the name on your Google account (used to suggest a nickname). We never receive your Google password, and we do not access your Google contacts, Drive, Gmail or any other Google data.
Nickname and countryYou create an accountShown publicly on the leaderboard (name and flag), together with the badge you choose to wear and whether you are on a play streak today.
Game progress: levels solved, stars, best times, hintsYou are signed inCloud save across reinstalls and phones, and the leaderboard (levels solved, highest level and stars are public).
PurchasesYou buy stars in the shopPayments are processed by Google Play, which handles your payment details; we never see them. Your star balance and badges are stored on your device.
Feedback you sendYou use "Send feedback"Your message, category, optional reply email, app version, Android version and highest level, so we can read and act on it.
Crash reportsThe app crashes (can be switched off)Technical details of the crash (device model, OS version, stack trace) and an anonymous account id, to fix bugs.
Usage statisticsYou play (can be switched off)Anonymous counts such as levels started, finished or left, time taken, and tutorial/sign-up completion, to balance difficulty. These are collected with an app-instance identifier by Google Firebase Analytics.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data, and we do not show ads or sell your data.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores accounts, cloud saves, leaderboard and feedback.
  • Google signs you in when you choose "Continue with Google", and processes purchases through Google Play.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) receives crash reports and usage statistics.

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it on servers outside your country.

One Path's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Play as a guest. No account is needed; your progress then stays on your phone.
  • Turn off "Send crash reports" and "Share usage statistics" in Settings → Privacy, and the daily reminder notification in Settings, at any time.
  • Change your nickname or country in Account.
  • Delete your account and data in the app (Account → Delete account) or see how to request deletion.

Retention

Account, progress and leaderboard data are kept until you delete your account. Feedback is kept for as long as it is useful for improving the game, and deleted on request. Crash and usage data are kept by Firebase for its standard retention period.

Children

One Path is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and protected by access rules on our servers. No online service is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and for material changes tell you in the app.
